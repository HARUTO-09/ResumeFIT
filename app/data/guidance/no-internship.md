Recruiters hiring freshers expect thin work history. What they look for instead is proof that you can finish things.

Use a "Projects" section as your main evidence and treat each project like a job: what the goal was, what you built, how you checked it worked. Other things that count as experience: open-source contributions, hackathons, freelance or volunteer technical work, teaching assistant or lab roles, research with a professor, and technical club work with a concrete outcome.

If you have none of these yet, one well-finished project with tests, a README and a live demo is worth more than five half-done ones. Do not rename coursework as an internship or invent a company; background checks and interviews expose it.
