---
title: Whether to write a summary or objective
source: Written for ResumeFit by the project author; original text, free to reuse in this project
version: 1
---
A generic objective such as "seeking a challenging position to utilise my skills" uses space and says nothing. Leave it out.

A short summary can help when it is specific and matches the role: who you are, your strongest relevant evidence, and what you are looking for, in two lines at most. Example: "Final-year computer science student. Built and deployed a resume-matching service with FastAPI, embeddings and SQL, evaluated against human labels. Looking for an entry-level AI engineering role."

Every claim in the summary must be backed further down the page. If your projects section already makes the case within the first third of the page, you do not need a summary at all.
