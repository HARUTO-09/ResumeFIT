---
title: Closing a skill gap honestly
source: Written for ResumeFit by the project author; original text, free to reuse in this project
version: 1
---
A missing skill on your match report is a to-do list, not a verdict. Sort the gaps into three groups.

You have it but did not show it: add it to the skills section and to a bullet that proves it. This is the fastest win.

You have something close: describe what you actually did, using the job description's term only if it is accurate. For example, SQLite experience supports "SQL", but not "PostgreSQL administration".

You do not have it: decide whether it is worth a small project. Pick the gaps the job description repeats most. Build the smallest thing that uses the skill for real, then add it. Until then, leave it off. Claiming a skill you cannot discuss costs more in the interview than the gap costs on paper.
