---
title: Explaining a project in an interview
source: Written for ResumeFit by the project author; original text, free to reuse in this project
version: 1
---
Expect "tell me about this project" for anything on your resume. Prepare a one-minute answer with four parts: the problem and who had it; your approach and why you chose it over a simpler one; the result, with a number and how you measured it; and what you would do next.

Then prepare for follow-ups: one design decision you would defend, one thing that went wrong and how you fixed or contained it, and one limit of the project you know about. Being open about a limit usually earns more credit than pretending there is none.

Practise explaining the system end to end, from input to output, without notes. If you cannot explain a part, either learn it or remove it from the resume.
