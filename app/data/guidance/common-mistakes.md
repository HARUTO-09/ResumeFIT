title: Common resume mistakes to remove
source: Written for ResumeFit by the project author; original text, free to reuse in this project

Check for these before sending a resume.

Vague verbs: "worked on", "helped with", "responsible for". Replace them with what you did.
Buzzwords without evidence: "passionate", "hard-working", "team player". Show it in a bullet instead.
Skills with no proof anywhere else on the page.
Spelling and grammar errors, and inconsistent tense or date formats.
An unprofessional email address or missing phone number.
Dense paragraphs instead of bullets.
Claims you cannot explain in an interview.
Personal details that are not needed for most applications, such as a photo, date of birth, marital status or full home address.
One resume sent unchanged to every role.

Read the page aloud once, then ask a classmate to find two things that are unclear.
