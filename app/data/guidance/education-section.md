title: Writing the education section
source: Written for ResumeFit by the project author; original text, free to reuse in this project
version: 1
---
List the degree, branch, institution and expected graduation year. Add your CGPA or percentage if it is above the cutoff most employers in your field use, or if the job description asks for it. If it is low, you may leave it off the resume, but answer honestly when asked.

Add "Relevant coursework" only when it supports the role, and keep it to four to six courses, for example Data Structures, DBMS, Operating Systems, Machine Learning. Mention a final-year project or thesis here only if it is not already under Projects.

Scholarships, ranks and academic awards fit in this section in one line each. Do not list every semester's marks.
