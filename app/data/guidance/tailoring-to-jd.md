---
title: Tailoring a resume to a job description
source: Written for ResumeFit by the project author; original text, free to reuse in this project
version: 1
---
Read the job description and mark the skills and duties it repeats; repetition signals what matters most. Then check your resume for evidence of each one.

Where you have the experience but used different words, switch to the job description's wording. If it says "REST APIs" and you wrote "web endpoints", change it. Where you have partial experience, say exactly what you did. Where you have none, do not add the keyword; plan how to get it instead.

Reorder before you rewrite: move the most relevant project and skills to the top. Keep one master resume with everything, and cut a tailored copy for each role. Tailoring changes emphasis and wording, never the facts.
