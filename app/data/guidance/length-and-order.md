---
title: Resume length and section order for students
source: Written for ResumeFit by the project author; original text, free to reuse in this project
version: 1
---
One page is right for almost every student and fresher. A second page is justified only by substantial internships or publications.

A common order for technical roles: name and contact line; skills; projects; experience (internships); education; then achievements or certifications. If you have a strong internship relevant to the role, put experience above projects. Education can move up if your institution or grades are a selling point.

Within each section, list items by relevance to the job first, then by date. Cut the objective statement unless it says something specific. Remove school-level details once you have a degree in progress, hobbies that say nothing about you, and the line "references available on request".
