title: Listing certifications and online courses
source: Written for ResumeFit by the project author; original text, free to reuse in this project
version: 1
---
Certificates help when they are relevant, recognised and backed by something you built. They do not replace projects.

List at most three or four, most relevant first, with the provider and year. Put the skill you gained into the skills section and, better, into a project. A course on SQL followed by a project that uses SQL is convincing; ten course certificates with no project are not.

Leave out certificates for attendance, very short tutorials and anything unrelated to the role. If a job description names a specific certification as required, say clearly whether you hold it or are preparing for it, with the expected date. Do not list a course you did not finish as completed.
